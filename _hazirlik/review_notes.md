# Species cards: items for expert review

## Name and spelling issues on the site
- **Eremias strauchi**: correct spelling is *strauchi* (Kessler, 1878); "strauchii" is wrong, so the merge is correct.
- **Mertensiella djanashvili**: should be *Mertensiella djanaschvilii* Tartarashvili & Bakradze, 1989. The Turkish name "Kafkas Semenderi" is the usual name for *M. caucasica*.
- **Darevskia uzzelli**: the name honours Thomas Uzzell, so the Turkish name should probably be "Uzzell Kertenkelesi".
- **Bombina variegata**: "Sarılı Kurbağa" may be a typo for "Sarı Karınlı Kurbağa".

## Low confidence
- **Bombina variegata**: not known from Türkiye. Either the photos were taken abroad, or they show *B. bombina* from Thrace.
- **Bufotes viridis**: Anatolian green toads are usually *B. sitibundus* (Dufresnes et al. 2019). If the photos are from Anatolia, change latin_valid.
- **Vipera meridionalis**: the split from *V. ammodytes* and where the Turkish populations belong (transcaucasiana in N Türkiye; status of W Anatolia and Thrace) are unsettled. Check the photo locality.
- **Darevskia obscura**: the Turkish and English names are proposed by me. Sources disagree on who raised it to species (Gabelaia et al. 2018 vs Arribas et al. 2022).

## Proposed or questionable Turkish names
- Darevskia obscura: "Koyu Kaya Kertenkelesi" (my proposal, based on the Latin name)
- Ommatotriton ophryticus: "Kuzey Bantlı Semenderi" (my proposal, from the English name "northern banded newt")
- Darevskia armeniaca: "Hemşin Kertenkelesi" looks odd; "Ermeni Kertenkelesi" may be the better match
- Darevskia adjarica: consider "Acara Kertenkelesi"
- Lacerta diplochondrodes: "Büyük Yeşil Kertenkele" has traditionally been used for *L. trilineata*
- Hemorrhois ravergieri ("Kocabaş Yılan"), Darevskia unisexualis ("Ağrı Kertenkelesi"): I could not find these names in the literature

## Taxonomy: check the photo locality
- Darevskia rudis, D. valentini: revised by Arribas et al. 2022 (D. mirabilis, D. spitzenbergerae and D. obscura raised to species; D. josefschmidtleri newly described)
- Mediodactylus danilewskii / M. orientalis: species boundaries in Türkiye after the 2018 split of the M. kotschyi complex
- Bufo bufo: in NE Türkiye this may be *B. verrucosissimus*
- Rana tavasensis: its status relative to *R. macrocnemis*
- Ablepharus anatolicus: a possible synonymy with *A. chernovi* has been suggested (2024)
- Mauremys caspica: rule out *M. rivulata* if the photos are from W Anatolia
- Hyla orientalis: rule out *H. savignyi* / *H. felixarabica* if the photos are from S or SE Türkiye
- Blanus strauchi: rule out *B. aporus* / *B. alexandri* if the photos are from S or SE Türkiye
